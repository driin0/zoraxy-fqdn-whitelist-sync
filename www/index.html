How the sync works
Each FQDN's A/AAAA records are resolved every interval and added to the whitelist, tagged fqdn-sync:<fqdn>.
Fail-closed: if a name stops resolving, its IPs are removed.
An FQDN resolving into a never-authorise range (e.g. 192.0.2.1) is treated as offline: nothing is whitelisted and its previous IP is removed at once.
Manually-added whitelist entries are never touched.
